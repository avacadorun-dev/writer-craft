## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Consistent approved lion identity
All active application and repository brand surfaces SHALL display the supplied lion writer from one preserved source. The full rounded tile SHALL remain opaque inside its silhouette; transparency SHALL be confined to its exterior. Native header presentation SHALL remain readable at 32 logical pixels in Light and Dark without changing toolbar/pane boundaries.

#### Scenario: Switching appearance or packaging platform
- **WHEN** an author switches appearance or installs a supported native package
- **THEN** the approved lion, quill, book and engraving SHALL remain consistent across header, window and application icon
- **AND** platform safe-area padding SHALL not shrink the visible header tile

### Requirement: Truthful current and historical visuals
The centered current banner SHALL use the lion. Current application screenshots SHALL come from the actual compiled source; archived released screenshots SHALL remain labeled historical evidence rather than being painted over.

#### Scenario: Reading current branding alongside an earlier release
- **WHEN** someone reads the README and release history
- **THEN** the current identity SHALL use the lion and earlier screenshots SHALL retain their actual source/version provenance
